Classic Tomato Basil Soup

Time: 30 mins Servings: 4
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 Yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 28 oz Canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups Vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup Heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup Fresh basil leaves
Instructions
  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat and sauté onion until soft.
  2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in crushed tomatoes and vegetable broth. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Blend the soup using an immersion blender until smooth.
  5. Stir in heavy cream and chopped basil. Season with salt and pepper.

Crispy Chickpea Salad

Time: 15 mins Servings: 2
Ingredients
  • 1 can Chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 Cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup Feta cheese, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
Instructions
  1. Pat chickpeas dry with a paper towel.
  2. Combine chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, and feta in a bowl.
  3. Whisk lemon juice and olive oil together, then pour over the salad.
  4. Toss gently to combine and serve cold.
